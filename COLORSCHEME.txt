THE PALETTE — five colours, nothing else
========================================

  #263640   dark slate    backgrounds, text, axes, ink
  #ffffff   white         panel backgrounds, bar edges
  #5096c0   blue          the model
  #e87878   red           the truth
  #e9c46a   yellow        what the model cannot see

Plus two neutrals, which are furniture rather than colour:

  #9e9e9e   grey          controls, ablations, reference lines
  #eeeeee   pale grey     raster bands, gridlines


WHAT EACH COLOUR MEANS, ON EVERY SLIDE
======================================

The deck's accents carry cell type, inside the rate scatters only:

  #e87878   red           excitatory
  #5096c0   blue          inhibitory

Two colours from the controls bar chart -- complementary and highly differentiable --
carry every "given versus inferred" pairing in the talk:

  #457b9d   steel blue    the teacher, the full connectome, the observed population
  #e07b2e   orange        the student, the learnt model, the unobserved population

The teacher's colour is deliberately the first bar of Figure 2: that bar is the teacher's
connectivity. The same pair then reads as observed versus unobserved in the sweeps, which
reinforces the story rather than confusing it -- blue is what you are given, orange is
what the model infers.

The remaining two control conditions keep their own fixed accents:

  #2a9d8f   teal          shuffled weights
  #8e6bb3   violet        shuffled topology

Furniture, never data:

  #263640   dark slate    text, axes, ink
  #9e9e9e   grey          noise ceilings, dimensionality markers, targeted cells
  #eeeeee   pale grey     raster bands, gridlines

The scaling-factor panel colours each factor by its presynaptic population -- red from
excitatory, blue from inhibitory, grey from mitral -- so the three factors onto inhibitory
cells read as a group.

The yellow accent (#e9c46a) is not used in the figures: on white it is too faint to carry
a series. It remains available for the slides themselves.

WHERE THIS LIVES IN CODE
========================

The five are defined once, in connectome_snns.visualization (SLIDE_INK, SLIDE_BLUE,
SLIDE_RED, SLIDE_YELLOW, and the neutrals). Figures never write a hex value: they import
the semantic names from common/style.py (truth, model, unobserved, control shades).

History: between 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-21 the figures drifted onto a twenty-colour
library palette, with blue meaning three different things across slides. This file
replaces that; the drift is in git history if a colour ever needs recovering.
